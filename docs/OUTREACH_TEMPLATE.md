# Design-Partner Outreach — University CubeSat Teams

> **Start in Phase 0 — this needs lead time.** One real team's verdict in the demo separates OrbitWarden from every other SSA dashboard entry. Target: send to 3–5 teams by ~Jul 28; aim for a 30-min trial session in Week 4 (Aug 22–28).

## Where to find teams

- The challenge **Discord** (space-interested students congregate there)
- University space-grant / CubeSat program pages (search: "university CubeSat program" + country)
- Teams with public TLEs already in orbit (check CelesTrak `GROUP=active` for university satellites — their NORAD IDs make the demo instantly personal: "here's *your* satellite's week")
- Student chapters: AIAA, IEEE, SEDA (Small Satellite community)
